How to play

It's minesweeper, but the grid never ends. Numbers show how many mines touch a cell. Open every safe cell to clear the level.

Around your grid is a ring of bigger blocks from the next level. You can open and flag them too. Cells count every mine they touch, whatever its size, so the numbers along your grid's edge include mines in the big blocks next to them.

When you clear a level the camera zooms out. Your grid turns into the centre 3×3 block of a new 9×9 grid, and anything you opened or flagged in the ring stays that way.

The same seed always gives the same mines, so you can share a seed and race a friend.
